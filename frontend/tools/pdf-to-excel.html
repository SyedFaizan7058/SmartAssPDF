Why use SmartAssPDF for PDF to Excel?

Turn financial statements, tabular invoices, and reports into editable spreadsheets without manually retyping data.

Quick Definition & Overview
PDF to Excel conversion parses tabular lines and textual records from PDF pages and maps them into native Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) worksheet rows and columns.
  • Converts PDF tables into Microsoft Excel .xlsx format
  • Auto-sizes column widths for easy data analysis
  • Free with client validation and temporary memory storage

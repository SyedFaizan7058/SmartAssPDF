ABOUT THIS TOOL

Why use SmartAssPDF for Scan to PDF?

Scan and digitize paper documents, receipts, contracts, IDs, and handwritten notes directly from your mobile camera or photos with zero software installation.

Quick Definition & Overview
Scan to PDF allows you to photograph physical documents using your mobile phone or webcam, fine-tune perspective corner crops, enhance text readability with high-contrast filters, and bundle all pages into a clean, printable PDF document.
  • On-Demand Camera Permission — never requests access until you click
  • Interactive 4-Corner Crop with touch-friendly quad corner handles
  • Original Photo, Document B&W, Magic Color, and Auto Enhance filters
  • High-speed client-side PDF compilation (100% free with zero latency)

How to Scan Documents to PDF

  1. 1. Start Camera or Upload Photos Click 'Start Scanning with Camera' to activate your rear document camera or webcam, or drop existing document photos.
  2. 2. Adjust 4-Corner Crop & Enhancement Drag the 4 corner handles to un-skew tilted photos. Choose between Original Photo, Magic Color, or Document B&W.
  3. 3. Arrange Pages & Create PDF Scan additional pages if needed, reorder thumbnails with ease, choose page size (A4, Letter, Legal), and download your PDF.

Key Studio Features

4-Corner Perspective Warp

Straightens documents photographed from angles. Drag the 4 corner handles to square the paper into a flat rectangular page.

5 Document Enhancement Filters

Select from Original Photo (default), Magic Color (whitened background), Document B&W (crisp text), Grayscale, or Auto Enhance.

Multi-Page Sequencer

Move pages left or right, rotate 90°, duplicate, or re-crop individual pages in your document sequence.

High-Speed Client Compiler

Builds PDFs directly inside your browser memory using PDF-Lib for instant downloads with zero server lag.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool request camera permissions automatically?
No. Your privacy is respected. Camera permissions are requested strictly on-demand when you click the 'Start Scanning with Camera' button.
Can I reorder or rotate pages before creating the PDF?
Yes! In the document sequencer stage, you can use the left/right arrows to change page order, rotate pages clockwise by 90°, or tap any page to re-crop it.
Are my scanned documents stored on your server?
No. Processing and PDF compilation happen locally inside your web browser. Nothing is permanently stored or saved on any database.

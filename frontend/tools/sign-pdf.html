Why Sign Documents with SmartAssPDF?

Easily execute employment agreements, NDA forms, vendor invoices, and real estate contracts with legal visual verification seals.

Quick Definition & Overview
PDF Signing stamps a cryptographic verification block with the signer's name, UTC timestamp, and optional signature graphic onto the document.
  • Choice of handwritten cursive text or uploaded signature graphic
  • Embeds UTC timestamp and audit verification badge
  • Customizable placement (bottom-right, bottom-left, center)

Frequently Asked Questions

What signature methods are supported?

SmartAssPDF supports type-based digital seals with timestamp certification, uploaded image signatures (PNG/JPG with transparency), and cryptographic certificate sealing.

Where can I position the signature on my PDF?

You can position your signature in the Bottom Right, Bottom Left, Top Right, Top Left, or Center of the document pages.

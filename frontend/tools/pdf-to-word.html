Frequently Asked Questions

Will my converted Word document be fully editable?
Yes. You can edit text, revise paragraphs, change font sizes, and re-save the document in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice.
Does this tool perform OCR on scanned image PDFs?
SmartAssPDF uses text-stream extraction from digital PDFs. Scanned bitmap image pages may require OCR software if no font glyphs are embedded.
Are my files secure?
Yes. Files are processed in temporary memory and scheduled for automatic hard deletion after 30 minutes.

ABOUT THIS TOOL

Why add watermarks to your PDF documents?

Watermarking protects confidential materials, establishes draft status, and deters unauthorized copying by adding clear text across your document pages.

Quick Definition & Overview
A PDF watermark is a semi-transparent text stamp placed diagonally or horizontally across document pages to signal confidentiality, copyright, or draft status.
  • Customizable text: CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, DO NOT COPY, or custom names
  • Multi-angle support: 45° diagonal, 0° horizontal, or 90° vertical
  • 100% free with automated 30-minute server cleanup

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the watermark text?
Yes. You can use standard presets (like CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, COPY) or type any custom company name or phrase you need.
Does watermarking degrade document quality?
No. Watermarks are rendered as crisp vector text streams without compressing or degrading the underlying PDF pages.
Are my uploaded files kept private?
Yes. Files are stored only temporarily in memory during conversion and automatically deleted after 30 minutes.
Can the watermark be removed after adding it?
The watermark is rendered as visual content embedded into the PDF pages, making it difficult to remove. It is not added as a separate removable layer, so standard layer-removal tools will not strip it.
Can I add an image watermark instead of text?
Currently the tool supports text watermarks with customizable font size, opacity, rotation angle, and color. Image watermark support is planned for a future update.

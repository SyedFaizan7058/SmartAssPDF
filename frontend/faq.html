Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct answers to common questions about file formats, conversion accuracy, privacy, limits, and technical specifications.

General & Access

What is SmartAssPDF?
SmartAssPDF is a modern, lightweight, privacy-focused online document processing suite. It provides 24+ essential PDF utilities including Mobile Scan to PDF with automatic 4-corner document detection, OCR PDF, document conversion (Word, Excel, JPG, PowerPoint), merging, splitting, compression, page rotation, and client-side encryption.
Is there any cost, subscription, or trial limit?
No. All tools on SmartAssPDF are 100% free forever with zero paywalls, credit cards, or hidden limits. You can process as many documents as you need without purchasing subscriptions.
Do I need to create an account or provide an email?
No account or login is required. We do not collect email addresses, passwords, or personal credentials. You can access every tool immediately from any device.
What devices and operating systems are supported?
SmartAssPDF runs directly inside any modern web browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS (iPhone & iPad), and Android. No software downloads or desktop installations are required.

Camera Scan & OCR Tools

How does the Scan to PDF auto 4-corner detection work?
The Scan to PDF tool utilizes real-time client-side OpenCV computer vision algorithms. When you point your camera at a document, receipt, or book, it tracks high-contrast geometric contours, performs Hough edge verification, and locks onto the four document corners. When held steady for 1.0s, it automatically captures the frame and corrects perspective distortion.
What is OCR PDF and why should I use it?
OCR (Optical Character Recognition) converts scanned paper photos and image-based PDFs into fully searchable documents. It generates an invisible, selectable text layer aligned with the visual characters, allowing you to highlight, search (Ctrl+F), and copy text from scanned PDFs.
Can I edit, rotate, or re-crop individual pages in multi-page scans?
Yes. After scanning pages, our multi-page sequencer displays compact page thumbnails. You can tap any thumbnail to open full-screen 4-corner re-cropping, apply document filters (Magic Color, B&W, Grayscale), rotate pages 90°, duplicate pages, or delete pages from the 3-dots menu before compiling the PDF.

File Conversion & Accuracy

What document formats can I convert?
SmartAssPDF supports bi-directional conversion between PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx), Images (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp), and HTML web documents.
Will tables and formatting be preserved during PDF to Word/Excel conversion?
Yes. Our backend conversion engine reconstructs paragraph hierarchies, typography, table cells, alignments, and embedded graphics into native editable Office elements with high visual fidelity.
What is Image to WebP conversion?
Image to WebP converts standard JPG and PNG images into the modern next-generation WebP image format, reducing file size by up to 70% while preserving sharpness and transparency for faster web loading.

Organize & Edit PDFs

How do I merge multiple PDF documents into one?
Open the Merge PDF tool, upload up to 20 PDF files, drag and drop the files into your desired sequential order, and click "Merge PDF". The combined document will be generated instantly.
Can I split a PDF or remove unwanted pages?
Yes. The Split PDF tool allows you to extract custom page ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-10) into separate documents, while the Remove Pages tool permanently deletes specific pages (e.g. 2, 4-6) and outputs a clean PDF.
How does the Compare PDFs tool detect differences?
The Compare PDFs tool analyzes text structures, modifications, and layout variations between two PDF versions, displaying changes side-by-side with clear visual difference indicators.

Privacy, Security & Limits

Are my documents stored permanently on your servers?
No. All uploaded documents are processed in isolated, sandboxed temporary directories and are permanently deleted after 30 minutes by an automated cron cleanup daemon. SmartAssPDF maintains zero permanent document storage.
What is the maximum upload file size?
The maximum individual file limit is 50 MB. Multi-file tools support up to 20 files per request.
What does the Sanitize PDF tool do?
Sanitize PDF scrubs hidden metadata, author names, creation dates, edit histories, GPS coordinates, JavaScript code, attachments, and embedded annotations to prevent accidental data leaks before public sharing.
Can I protect or unlock password-protected PDFs?
Yes. The Protect PDF tool applies industry-standard 256-bit AES encryption to lock documents with user passwords, while the Unlock PDF tool removes encryption restrictions from PDFs you own.
Can I add electronic signatures with the Sign PDF tool?
Yes. The Sign PDF tool provides an interactive signature pad where you can draw your signature, type it, or upload a signature image and position it securely anywhere on your PDF.

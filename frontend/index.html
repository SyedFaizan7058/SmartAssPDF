FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about SmartAssPDF workflows and privacy.

Is SmartAssPDF completely free to use?
Yes. SmartAssPDF is powered by open-source technologies (Java, Spring Boot, PDFBox, Apache POI, PyMuPDF) and does not require paid subscriptions or credits for any of its 19 core tools.
Do I need to sign up or create an account?
No account or registration is required. You can select a tool, upload files, configure options, and download your result instantly.
What is the maximum file size limit?
The default file limit is 50 MB per file, and multi-file tools like Merge PDF and JPG to PDF support up to 20 files per request.
How are my files handled and deleted?
Uploaded documents are placed into temporary job directories on the server during processing. A scheduled cleanup service automatically deletes job files after 30 minutes.
Are conversions pixel-perfect?
Conversions extract text, structures, and tables cleanly for standard digital documents. Extremely complex layouts, scanned OCR images, or custom proprietary fonts may require minor manual review.

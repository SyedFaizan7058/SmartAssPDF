Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Overview

SmartAssPDF is committed to protecting your privacy while delivering fast, frictionless document utilities. This policy explains how we utilize browser cookies, local storage mechanisms, and third-party advertising services.

2. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Local Storage is a browser feature that allows websites to store key-value data persistently without sending it to the server on every request.

3. How SmartAssPDF Uses Browser Storage

We prioritize minimal data collection. Specifically:

  • Theme Preference (Local Storage): We store your choice of Dark or Light mode under smartasspdf_theme. This ensures zero layout flicker (FOUC) and is stored exclusively in your browser.
  • Essential Operations: We do not require account creation, session cookies, or user tracking to perform document conversions.
  • Temporary Job Directories: Uploaded files are assigned isolated UUIDs on our processing server and automatically deleted within 30 minutes. No document content is stored in cookies.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

To keep all 16 PDF tools 100% free without charging subscription fees, SmartAssPDF may partner with advertising networks such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to SmartAssPDF and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings. However, this may affect how you are able to interact with various websites.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us via our Contact Page or review our Privacy Policy.

How to Convert JPG and PNG Images to PDF Online

Scanned receipts, whiteboard notes, and photo documents are easier to send, print, and archive when compiled into a standard PDF.

SmartAssPDF Editorial 3 min read Updated 2026-08-20
Quick Answer Summary
To convert JPG images to PDF, upload one or more image files to the SmartAssPDF JPG to PDF tool, verify the order, and click Convert. The engine scales each image onto standard A4 pages and produces an instant PDF download.
  • Supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG image formats
  • Auto-centers and scales images preserving original proportions
  • Batch combine multiple images into a multi-page PDF

Advantages of Converting Images to PDF

  • Universal Printing: Images often print with unpredictable margins or scaling on different printers. PDF enforces standard A4 margins.
  • Single File Packaging: Send a 10-page receipt set as 1 clean PDF rather than 10 separate image attachments.
  • Cross-Platform Compatibility: Open effortlessly on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows without specialized image software.

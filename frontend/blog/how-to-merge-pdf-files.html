How to Merge Multiple PDF Files into One Document

Combining multiple individual documents into a single clean PDF binder makes sharing, printing, and archiving seamless.

SmartAssPDF Editorial 3 min read Updated 2026-08-20
Quick Answer Summary
To merge PDF files, upload two or more PDFs to the SmartAssPDF Merge PDF tool, arrange them in your desired order, and click Merge. The engine concatenates the page trees and returns a single unified PDF.
  • Combine up to 20 PDF documents in a single operation
  • Drag and drop reordering of files before merging
  • Preserves original page sizes, orientations, and bookmarks

When Should You Merge PDF Documents?

Consolidating disparate files into a single unified binder is essential for:

  • Job Applications: Combining your resume, cover letter, and portfolio samples into one submission.
  • Financial & Legal Records: Grouping monthly bank statements, receipts, and tax filings into one annual binder.
  • Academic Submissions: Merging research papers, appendixes, and bibliography citations into one document.

Step-by-Step Merging Guide

  1. 1. Select Your Files Open Merge PDF and select at least two PDF documents.
  2. 2. Arrange File Order Review the file list. Files will be joined in top-to-bottom sequence. Remove or re-upload if needed.
  3. 3. Merge and Save Click "Process Merge PDF". Your merged document is produced in seconds with dynamic naming (e.g. file1_merged.pdf).

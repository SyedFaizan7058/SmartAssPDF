Privacy Policy

SmartAssPDF is designed around privacy, temporary processing, and automatic file cleanup.

1. Uploaded Documents

When you select a tool and click Process, your chosen file is transmitted to the backend processing service. A temporary job directory is created for the duration of the operation to execute the conversion or manipulation task.

2. Automatic File Deletion

Temporary job directories and converted outputs are scheduled for automatic deletion by our background cleanup service after 30 minutes. We do not maintain a permanent document archive.

3. No Account Database

SmartAssPDF operates without a user account database. Active jobs and temporary file paths exist solely in application memory while being processed.

4. Passwords in Protect & Unlock

Passwords provided to the Protect PDF or Unlock PDF utilities are passed exclusively to transient PDFBox memory during encryption/decryption. Passwords are never logged or stored.

5. User Responsibility

You should only upload documents you have authorization to process. Always keep original copies of your important files.
